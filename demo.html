CongressContactFinder demo

Sample embeds that resolve a current member’s official contact website, then try to show that live page in an iframe (with a fallback when the site blocks embedding).

1. Drop-in block (data-name)

Sample name: Bernie Sanders

<div data-congress-contact data-name="Bernie Sanders" data-iframe-height="700"></div>
<script src="embed.js"></script>

2. iframe (query param)

<iframe src="/path/to/embed.html?name=Alexandria%20Ocasio-Cortez"
  title="Congress contact" style="width:100%;height:780px;border:0"></iframe>

3. Live lookup
