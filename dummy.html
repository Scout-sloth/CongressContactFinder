A sample host page for trying CongressContactFinder embed snippets.

City councils across the region are asking residents to contact their senators and representatives before the next appropriations vote. This page is a stand-in for a normal advocacy site — the yellow boxes mark where the contact embeds are plugged in.

Each embed looks up the member’s official contact URL. If framing is allowed, it shows the live page in a tall iframe; if the site blocks embedding (most .gov offices), you get a prominent “Open contact form / website” button instead — never a blank frame.
